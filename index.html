MODEL

Calendar loss = a(V,T)·t0.75, a = (7.543·V − 23.75)·106·e−6976/T; cycle loss = (7.348·10−3(V̄−3.667)² + 7.6·10−4 + 4.081·10−3·DoD)·√Ah (Schmalstieg et al. 2014, NMC). Stress is integrated every 15 minutes from the bike's state of charge and temperature with the equivalent-time method, so parked-full hours, heat and riding each add their share.

A rider-specific scale k is fitted from health readings at charge sessions (grid posterior, lognormal prior). The band is then widened by split conformal calibration on simulated riders whose calendar and cycle aging deviate from the model by ±20%, so the 80% band holds even when the physics model is off.
